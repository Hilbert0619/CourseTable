**CC0 1.0 (public domain)**: it may be copied, changed and shipped in the app without asking or giving credit.
We list the sources anyway. `tools/make_sounds.py` (run by the manual `Sounds` workflow) cuts each one into a
seamless loop at matched loudness; the start and length used are in `sources.json`.
